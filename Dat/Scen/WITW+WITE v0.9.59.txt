WITW+WITE v0.9.59

July 1943 to July 1945. Eastern and Western Front campaign. Original scenario by yyuegu. Unofficial continuation. Both sides Human recommended.

71 Italian mainland ground units and HQs start frozen with zero movement. They can be released individually by enemy proximity or attack. Sicily and Sardinia have their original garrisons.

113 Western Allied divisions can reform once after permanent exit (including voluntary disbandment). After ten weeks, find the empty cadre in Soviet Reserves. Raise Max TOE and use Refit. Set Liverpool (72,158) as the shared Reserve TB Arrival Hex and order deployment. Reassign HQs as needed.

Covered Rifle Divisions can take one direct support unit. Ports expanded (Gela 2, 10 British and 35 mainland ports +1). Western Axis field units outside Atlantic Wall garrisons are scheduled to unlock on turn 16. 349 shared troop ships at start. Allied manpower grants total 1,960,000. Western units gain morale protection. British parachute brigades stay independent.

Western Europe, Italy and the Balkans are fought on the map. Regional capitulation scripts for these campaigns are disabled. Most former theater formations enter Axis Reserves on their scheduled dates.

Western Allied aircraft replacements use Western aircraft types. B-17G deliveries start in September 1943 and P-51B deliveries in November 1943.

Axis Reserves units can be assigned under the normal deployment rules as soon as they arrive. Scheduled map transfers and disbandments still apply.

Western Allied air headquarters use the campaign Western morale adjustment. At the July 1943 start their standard-difficulty recovery target is 65.

German western and Italian theater replacements: 3,000 extra men at start, then dated allocations through April 1945, credited to the German national pool.
Normandy reinforcements: western allocations are 6,710 men on July 15, 1944 and 10,000 on August 1. Both events add manpower to the German national pool.

Western Allied strategic bombing is directed by the player. Normandy breakthroughs follow play on the map. Starting industrial damage in the Ruhr is applied on turn one.
