WITW+WITE v0.9.63

July 1943 to July 1945. Eastern and Western Front campaign. Original
scenario by yyuegu. Unofficial continuation. Both sides Human
recommended.

71 Italian mainland ground units and HQs start frozen with zero
movement. They can be released individually by enemy proximity or
attack. Sicily and Sardinia have their original garrisons.

113 Western Allied divisions can reform once after permanent exit
(including voluntary disbandment). After ten weeks, find the empty
cadre in Soviet Reserves. Raise Max TOE and use Refit. Set Liverpool
(72,158) as the shared Reserve TB Arrival Hex and order deployment.
Reassign HQs as needed.

Covered Rifle Divisions can take one direct support unit. Ports
expanded (Gela 2, 10 British and 35 mainland ports +1). Western Axis
formations in France, Belgium and the Netherlands outside Atlantic
Wall garrisons start unfrozen and use normal movement rules from
their first turn. Atlantic Wall garrisons retain their 26-turn
freeze. Allied manpower grants total 1,960,000. Western units gain
morale protection. British parachute brigades stay independent.

Western Europe, Italy and the Balkans are fought on the map.
Regional capitulation scripts for these campaigns are disabled. Former theater air groups are stationed on the map. Ground reserve schedules are retained.

Western Allied aircraft replacements use Western aircraft types.
B-17G deliveries start in September 1943 and P-51B deliveries in
November 1943.

Axis Reserves units can be assigned under the normal deployment
rules as soon as they arrive. Scheduled map transfers and
disbandments still apply.

Western Allied air headquarters use the campaign Western morale
adjustment. At the July 1943 start their standard-difficulty
recovery target is 65.

German western and Italian theater replacements: 3,000 extra men at
start, then dated allocations through April 1945, credited to the
German national pool.
Normandy reinforcements: western allocations are 6,710 men on July
15, 1944 and 10,000 on August 1. Both events add manpower to the
German national pool.

Western Allied strategic bombing is directed by the player. Normandy
breakthroughs follow play on the map. Starting industrial damage in
the Ruhr is applied on turn one.

Grossdeutschland uses the stock July 1943 panzergrenadier
organization with its existing equipment.

Britain and the Mediterranean share 600 troop ships and 2,000 cargo
ships at the start of the campaign, shown in the Ladoga column.
Soviet troop ships and cargo ships in the other sea zones match the
1943 Campaign: Baltic 20/100, Black Sea 25/100, Azov 5/20, Caspian
5/20.

Western depots and revised Sherman W, reconnaissance and maritime aircraft production.

Western rail commands, UK Home Forces, revised Allied equipment and air deployment.

126 later Western Allied ground units start frozen in Britain or North Africa.
