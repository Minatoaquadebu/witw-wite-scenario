WITW+WITE v0.9.42
July 3, 1943 - July 6, 1945 (maximum 106 turns)

Eastern Front, Western Europe and Mediterranean campaign.
Player 2 commands Soviet and Western Allied forces. Both sides Human is
recommended; Western strategic AI is incomplete. Nation 12 preserves
Western control but shares manpower, vehicles, supplies and leader pools.
Soviet replacement commanders may appear.

Dated roster: 1010 ground and 198 air arrivals from the official WiTW
campaign. Some future units are preplaced and frozen. This is not a claim
to every historical formation. Historical officers are preassigned where
compatible; later command handovers are incomplete.

Finite manpower and dated overseas equipment/logistics allocations are
scenario estimates informed by historical shortages, not exact statistics.
Opening stocks are repaired. Six existing airborne division thirds now
use regiment/brigade TOEs; intact divisions cannot drop as a whole.

Four default Western opening air orders are included. Husky airborne targets are preset; launch remains a player choice. Two airborne
thirds have 50 preparation points. Actual new air orders and drops still
need runtime checks.

Native NO EARLY END retains Berlin and expiry endings. The 163 victory
cities do not set custom final grades. Balance and long stability are
untested. Requires WiTE2 v1.05.02. See included README for limitations.
