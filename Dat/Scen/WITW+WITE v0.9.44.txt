WITW+WITE v0.9.44

July 3, 1943 - July 6, 1945. A combined Eastern and Western Front campaign.
Original scenario by yyuegu; unofficial community continuation and repairs.

The Soviet side also controls the Western Allies. Nation 12 is a
compatibility workaround: coalition manpower, vehicles, stocks and leader
pools are shared. Both sides Human is recommended; Western strategic AI
remains unverified.

Historical ground and air arrivals from the official WiTW campaign are
restored, with frozen preplacement where required. Selected leaders, Western
victory points, dated overseas production and finite replacement allowances
are added. Allied opening stocks and graded ground morale/experience repairs
are retained.

II US Corps and Seventh Army stage at Malta; 15th Army Group at Bizerte. TF
81 and US 1st Division target the clear beach east of Gela (149,293),
adjoining the45th Division beach, allowing a temporary port after success.
Move ground HQs onward after landing for support commitment within five
hexes. Two opening ground-attack orders target southern Sicily: unit attacks
and interdiction on alternating days. Actual execution requires native
verification.

Airborne detachments retain preparation and historical targets; orders are
manual. No automatic drop or assured beachhead survival. Native Berlin/date
end rules remain. Start a new game for all changes; see README for limits.
