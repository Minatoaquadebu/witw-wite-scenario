WITW+WITE v0.9.38 Allied Symbols
July 3, 1943 - July 6, 1945 (maximum106 turns)

Optional Visual Variant
Install the accompanying Dat/Art/Units files before choosing this scenario.
Green panels distinguish common Western Allied unit symbols from Soviet
symbols. Nationality backgrounds are unchanged. Air and amphibious HQs,
some specialized types and the smallest zoom retain their original display.
All gameplay data is identical to the standard v0.9.38 scenario.

The campaign combines Eastern Front, Western Europe and Mediterranean map
operations. Player2 commands Soviet and Western Allied forces. Both sides
set to Human is recommended; Western strategic AI remains unresolved.

Selected reinforcement fixes and finite extra manpower allocations are
included, but the full Allied historical roster is NOT restored. Some future
units are preplaced and frozen. Western units use nation12 for control and
share Soviet manpower, production and leader pools. Soviet replacement
commanders can appear. Newly scheduled arrivals and manpower delivery have
had structural checks only; they are not fully runtime verified.

Native NO EARLY END rules still retain Berlin-related and scenario-expiry
endings. There are163 victory cities; VP do not define custom final grades.
Full-campaign balance and stability remain untested. WiTE2 v1.05.02.
